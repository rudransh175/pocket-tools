CGPA Converter

CGPA ↔ percentage using the CBSE × 9.5 formula, plus a quick semester SGPA average.

CGPA → Percentage

—

Formula (CBSE): Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. Some universities use a different multiplier — check your institution's rule if this is for an official form.

Percentage → CGPA

—

Reverse of the same formula: CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5.

Semester SGPA average → CGPA

—

Simple average of the SGPAs you enter (equal weight per semester). Percentage shown uses × 9.5.
